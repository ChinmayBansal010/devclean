# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want `devclean` to be an open, respectful, and technically serious project. Be constructive, assume good intent, and keep disagreements focused on ideas and implementation details.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Welcome different experience levels
- Give actionable, specific feedback
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, and dismissive behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive.
